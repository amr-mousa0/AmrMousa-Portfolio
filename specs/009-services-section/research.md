# Phase 0: Research & Resolutions

This document captures the resolutions to the unknowns and "NEEDS CLARIFICATION" points identified during the specification phase.

## 1. Services List
- **Decision**: The final list of services will focus on the technical capabilities of a Data & Marketing Analyst, using highly searched keywords.
  1. Dashboard Analysis & BI
  2. SQL Data Expert
  3. Advanced Excel Solutions
  4. Data-Driven Media Buying
  5. Social Media Specialist
  6. CRM & Data Management
- **Rationale**: This balances the user's deep technical expertise in data analysis with their marketing capabilities, matching what clients and recruiters actually search for.

## 2. Section Placement
- **Decision**: Place the "Services" section immediately after the "Experience" section and before the "Projects" section.
- **Rationale**: A portfolio builds authority first. Showing experience establishes trust, making the user "ready to buy" when they reach the services section.

## 3. Call-to-Action (CTA) Behavior
- **Decision**: Clicking a service card opens an elegant, glassmorphic Modal with detailed information. The modal will contain a final CTA button that scrolls the user to the Contact section.
- **Rationale**: This aligns with the "Lux Cinematic" design style. It provides space to explain the value of each service (e.g., what's included in a package) without cluttering the main grid, and still drives conversion towards the contact form.
