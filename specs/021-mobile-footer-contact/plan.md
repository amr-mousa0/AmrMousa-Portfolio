## Summary

This feature implements a global luxury footer at the bottom of the landing page for all viewports (desktop and mobile) to provide structural completeness, a brief bio, localized links, and social connections. 
On mobile viewports:
1. The footer will render direct call and WhatsApp buttons, styled in the portfolio's "quiet luxury" theme (replacing/enhancing the existing bottom bar pattern).
2. The contact form on the home page will be optimized for mobile by making it collapsible/compact, reducing vertical scroll clutter while preserving full message submission capabilities.
