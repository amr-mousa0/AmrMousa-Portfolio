# Design System — Amr Mousa Portfolio

## Visual Identity
- **Theme**: Dark Cinematic. High contrast with deep surfaces and vibrant accents.
- **Rhetoric**: Technical-Premium. A blend of clean lines, data-inspired patterns, and elegant typography.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#1A1A1A` (Dark Gray)
- **Surface**: `#292929` (Elevation 1)
- **Accent A (Primary)**: `#004D61` (Teal) — Used for data, logic, and primary actions.
- **Accent B (Secondary)**: `#822659` (Maroon/Berry) — Used for human insight, marketing, and highlights.
- **Text**: `#F0F0F0` (High Emphasis), `#999999` (Muted).

## Typography
- **Primary (UI/Body)**: `Poppins`. Modern, geometric, and highly readable.
- **Secondary (Display/Italic)**: `Playfair Display`. Used for quotes and emphasis to add authority and a "prestige" feel.
- **Brand (Logo)**: `Dancing Script`. Used sparingly for the "Amr Mousa" signature feel.

## Layout & Motion
- **Split-Screen**: Desktop layout favors a fixed visual side and a scrolling content side.
- **Micro-interactions**: 
  - **Pulse**: Used for CTAs to drive attention.
  - **Bounce**: Used for navigation indicators (Learn More).
  - **Glassmorphism**: Subtle use of `backdrop-filter` for floating UI elements.
- **Spacing Scale**: 14px base radius for containers. Ample padding (80px+) for sections.

## Components
- **Hero**: Split-screen with a wavy mask image and centralized CTAs.
- **Cards**: Surface-colored cards with 14px radius and subtle hover scales.
- **Indicators**: Minimalist animated cues for scrolling.
